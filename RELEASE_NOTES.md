# Nudgy 0.1.1

A sleepy panda in armor that sits on your screen and keeps you on task. Windows 10/11.

**Install:** run `Nudgy_0.1.1_x64-setup.exe`. No admin needed. Windows may show "Windows protected your PC" because the installer isn't signed yet: click **More info → Run anyway**.

## What it does
- **Focus sessions:** set a goal and length ("Exam prep, 2 h").
- **Drift detection:** notices YouTube, games or social media and nudges you after a couple of minutes (adjustable).
- **Knows the difference:** a lecture on your topic counts as study.
- **Away-from-screen goals:** for "reading a book", any PC use counts as drift.
- **Quick replies:** "Back to work", "5-min break", "I'm actually working" (it learns).
- **3 personalities:** Gentle, Coach, Savage.
- **Reminders:** select text with a date, press Ctrl+Alt+D.
- **Weekly report** with streaks and a share image.
- Quiet during calls and presentations. **Private:** nothing leaves your PC.

## Browser extension (optional)
Lets Nudgy see which website you're on. It comes with the app: in Nudgy open **Settings → Browser → Open extension folder** and follow the steps shown there. Full guide: [EXTENSION.md](https://github.com/quagmire02/nudgy/blob/master/EXTENSION.md).

## Known issues
- Over a fullscreen game or video during a session, the panda stays up to nudge you. Games in *exclusive* fullscreen mode draw over everything, so there the nudge shows when you alt-tab or leave the game; borderless/windowed fullscreen (the default in most games, including Fortnite) works.
- Windows notifications don't show while Windows' Do Not Disturb or full-screen mode is on (the panda still shows reminders).
