## Description (paste into the page)

**Meet Nudgy:** a small panda in too-big knight armor. Lazy by nature, but on duty anyway. It sits in the corner of your screen while you work and catches you when you drift.

### How it works
1. Tell it your goal: *"Exam prep, 2 hours"*.
2. Work. The panda dozes in the corner.
3. Wander off to YouTube, a game or Instagram, and after a couple of minutes it pipes up: *"Instagram, 6 minutes. The tabs won this round."*

### Features
- 🎯 **Smart drift detection:** a lecture on your topic counts as study; cat videos don't.
- 📖 **Away-from-screen mode:** reading a book? Any PC use counts as drift.
- 💬 **Quick replies:** Back to work · 5-min break · I'm actually working (it learns).
- 🎭 **3 personalities:** Gentle, Coach, or Savage (it roasts you, lovingly).
- 📜 **Reminders from anything:** select "Quiz on 5 Dec, 10am" anywhere, press Ctrl+Alt+D.
- 📊 **Weekly report:** focus hours, top distractions, streaks, and a shareable card.
- 🤫 **Polite:** quiet during calls and presentations. Snooze and breaks anytime.
- 🔒 **Private:** everything stays on your PC. No account, no tracking, no ads.

### Install
Download and run the installer (no admin needed). Windows may say *"Windows protected your PC"*. The app isn't code-signed yet, so click **More info → Run anyway**.

**Optional browser extension** (lets Nudgy see which website you're on): it comes with the app. Open Nudgy → **Settings → Browser → Open extension folder** and follow the 4 steps shown there. Full guide for Chrome, Edge, Brave and Firefox: https://github.com/quagmire02/nudgy/blob/master/EXTENSION.md
